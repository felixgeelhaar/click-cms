# Edit your website — English

[Deutsch](editor-de.md) · [Full handbook](handbook-en.md)

This guide is for people who change words and pictures on a website. You do not need to install anything or write code. Ask the person who manages your website for your sign-in address, username and password.

## Change some text

1. Open your sign-in address and sign in. Choose a new password if asked.
2. Click **Pages** in the menu.
3. Find the page you want and click **Edit**.
4. Find the text you want to change. A page is divided into **sections**: smaller parts such as a heading, a picture or a block of text. Open the section you need.
5. Change the words and click **Save**.
6. Open the preview and read your changes.
7. When the page is ready for visitors, click **Publish**. If this button is missing, ask the person who approves your work to publish it.
8. Open the public website and check the result.

**Finished when:** visitors can see the right words on the right page.

## What do Save and Publish mean?

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Keeps your changes as a draft. Visitors do not see these changes yet. |
| Preview | Lets you check the draft before visitors see it. |
| **Publish** | Makes the saved changes visible to visitors. |
| Take down | Hides the page from visitors but keeps it for you to edit. |
| **Delete** | Removes the page. Ask for help if you only want to hide it. |

If the page is already live, saving a new draft leaves the previous public version visible. Check the message at the top of the editor to see whether changes are waiting to be published.

## Change a picture

1. Open the page and find the section containing the picture.
2. Open its image chooser. Select a picture or upload one if your account allows it.
3. Add a short description of what a meaningful picture shows. For example: “Two team members working in the laboratory.”
4. Preview the page. Check that the important part of the picture is visible on a phone too.
5. Save and publish when ready.

Keep the original picture somewhere safe. Do not delete a picture from **Media** just because you removed it from one page: another page may still use it.

## Edit another language

If your website has more than one language, choose the language you want before changing the text. Translate the relevant fields, then save, preview and publish that version. Open the public page in that language to check it. Repeat for each language you need to change.

## Add a news item or similar entry

Some websites keep news, people or events under **Collections**. A collection is simply a list of similar items.

1. Click **Collections** and choose the list you need.
2. Create an entry or open an existing one.
3. Fill in the fields. Complete any fields marked as required.
4. Save, preview where available, and publish or ask for approval.
5. Check where the item appears on your website.

The names and fields depend on your website. Ask your website manager if you cannot find the right list.

## Fix a mistake

If you have not published yet, correct the draft and save again. If you need an earlier saved version, open the page history and check the version before restoring it. Check the restored draft before publishing.

If a message says someone else changed the page, copy your unfinished text somewhere safe and ask them to agree the changes with you. Reload the latest page before continuing.

## When something does not work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| You cannot sign in | Check your sign-in address and username. Ask your website manager to reset a lost password. |
| There is no Publish button | Ask the person who approves your work. Your account may only prepare drafts. |
| The website still shows the old text | Check that you published the correct language. Refresh the public page. If it stays old, contact your website manager. |
| A new page is missing from the menu | Ask an administrator to add its menu link. |
| A picture will not upload | Read the error message. Try a smaller suitable image, or ask your website manager. |

Send your website manager the page address, what you tried and the message you saw. Never send your password.

## Try it with help

Ask your website manager for a practice website and a sample page. Change one sentence, save, preview and publish it. Check the public page together. Do not practice deleting pages on the live website.
